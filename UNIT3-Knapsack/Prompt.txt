Create a clear and professional Dynamic Programming table visualization for the 0/1 Knapsack Problem with 4 items and maximum capacity W = 10.

Input Parameters:
- Total Knapsack Capacity: 10
- Item 1: Weight = 1, Profit = 10
- Item 2: Weight = 2, Profit = 20
- Item 3: Weight = 4, Profit = 35
- Item 4: Weight = 5, Profit = 45

Design & Visualization Requirements:
1. Matrix Layout:
   - Rows: Items 0 to 4 (Item 0 = Base case initialized to 0, followed by Items 1 to 4 with their weight & profit annotations).
   - Columns: Knapsack sub-capacities from 0 to 10.
2. Data Values:
   - Display all calculated DP table values dp[i][w] computed via bottom-up recurrence:
     dp[i][w] = max(dp[i-1][w], profit[i-1] + dp[i-1][w-weight[i-1]])
3. Visual Styling & Callouts:
   - Use an academic presentation style (clean navy/slate headers, high contrast legible fonts, crisp borders).
   - Prominently highlight the optimal backtracking route through the matrix cells that lead to the solution.
   - Highlight the final optimal cell dp[4][10] = 90 with a special border/accent.
   - Include a summary banner indicating:
     * Selected Subset: Items {1, 3, 4}
     * Total Weight: 1 + 4 + 5 = 10 / 10
     * Maximum Profit: 10 + 35 + 45 = 90
